# Agentic AI

Fundamental of Agentic AI is a course that moves from basic programming concepts to building and evaluating tool-using AI agents. The focus is on achieving conceptual fluency in AI systems, enabling students to build, evaluate, and lead technical AI work.

## Key facts
- Fundamental of Agentic AI is not a survey course about AI, and it is not a prompt-engineering workshop. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Course Objective]])
- In seven sessions we go from “what is a variable” to building and evaluating a tool-using AI agent — covering programming foundations, full-stack software systems, machine learning, deep learning and transformers, LLM behavior and retrieval, and agent architecture. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Course Objective]])
- The focus is conceptual fluency that survives the tools. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Course Objective]])
- Concretely, the course targets Level 2 fluency: you do not need a PhD to reason about AI systems, but knowing how to use ChatGPT is not a differentiator either. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Course Objective]])
- Assignments are individual. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Graded Assignments]])
- You are expected to use AI coding agents (see the AI policy below); you are also expected to understand and be able to explain everything you submit. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Graded Assignments]])
- The distinction this course cares about is not “did a human or an AI write this.” It is “does the person submitting it understand the system well enough to be responsible for it.” ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › AI / LLM Policy]])
- Every assignment is designed to be built with an AI coding agent. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › AI / LLM Policy]])
