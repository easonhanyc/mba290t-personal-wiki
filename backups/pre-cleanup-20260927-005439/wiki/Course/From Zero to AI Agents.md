# From Zero to AI Agents

This seven-class course guides students from basic programming concepts to building and evaluating tool-using AI agents. It covers a broad range of topics including machine learning, deep learning, LLM behavior, and agent architecture.

## Key facts
- Fundamental of Agentic AI is not a survey course about AI, and it is not a prompt-engineering workshop. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Course Objective]])
- In seven sessions we go from “what is a variable” to building and evaluating a tool-using AI agent — covering programming foundations, full-stack software systems, machine learning, deep learning and transformers, LLM behavior and retrieval, and agent architecture. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Course Objective]])
- The course targets Level 2 fluency: you do not need a PhD to reason about AI systems, but knowing how to use ChatGPT is not a differentiator either. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Course Objective]])
- A student who successfully completes this course will be able to read, modify, test, and debug real code, and explain what a program does before running it. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Learning Outcomes]])
- The course is assessed entirely through attendance and five graded assignments. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Class Schedule]])
- Attendance is required and is worth 20% of your grade. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Attendance]])
- Assignments 1–4 are the end-of-class deliverables for Classes 2 through 5. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Graded Assignments]])
- Assignment 5 is the course capstone and spans Classes 6 and 7. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Graded Assignments]])
- Every assignment is scored out of 10 using the same broad framework. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › How Assignments Are Graded]])
- The grading framework includes Deliverable quality, Testing & evaluation, and Working result. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › How Assignments Are Graded]])
- Performance will be graded with Attendance at 20%, Assignments 1–4 (lowest score dropped) at 50%, and Assignment 5 at 30%. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Grading]])
- Class 7 is on Tue 10/6 and covers Multi-Agent Systems, Ops & Safety. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Class Schedule]])
