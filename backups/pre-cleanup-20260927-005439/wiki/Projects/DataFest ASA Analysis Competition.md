# DataFest ASA Analysis Competition

This project involved analyzing data from the American Bar Association's legal advice platform over 48 hours. The team found that categories with the fewest cases had the highest non-response rates, which contradicted the initial expectation that busy areas were falling behind.

## Key facts
- The American Bar Association platform connects low-income clients with volunteer attorneys for free advice. ([[raw/website/projects/datafest-2023#1. The brief, and the clock|website/projects/datafest-2023.md › 1. The brief, and the clock]])
- The brief asked the team to advise the ABA and its state partners on general trends, identify where expertise was short, and help prepare volunteers for the questions coming at them. ([[raw/website/projects/datafest-2023#1. The brief, and the clock|website/projects/datafest-2023.md › 1. The brief, and the clock]])
- Education carried the largest share of unresolved questions across Southern states. ([[raw/website/projects/datafest-2023#3. What the data said|website/projects/datafest-2023.md › 3. What the data said]])
- Juvenile and individual rights had the highest proportion of non-response nationally. ([[raw/website/projects/datafest-2023#3. What the data said|website/projects/datafest-2023.md › 3. What the data said]])
- Family and children, the single largest category by volume, had a relatively low proportion of non-response. ([[raw/website/projects/datafest-2023#3. What the data said|website/projects/datafest-2023.md › 3. What the data said]])
- The worst single wait was work, employment and unemployment questions in South Carolina, at a median of 26 days. ([[raw/website/projects/datafest-2023#3. What the data said|website/projects/datafest-2023.md › 3. What the data said]])
- The data said the opposite of the intuitive read, which was that the busiest areas of law fall furthest behind. ([[raw/website/projects/datafest-2023|website/projects/datafest-2023.md]])
- The categories with fewer cases were the ones going unanswered. ([[raw/website/projects/datafest-2023#4. The finding that inverted the expectation|website/projects/datafest-2023.md › 4. The finding that inverted the expectation]])
- A retrieval system that surfaces similar past cases can only be as good as the corpus it draws from. ([[raw/website/projects/datafest-2023#5. Turning it on our own system|website/projects/datafest-2023.md › 5. Turning it on our own system]])
- The submission carried two worked retrievals: a family law question and an education question from South Carolina. ([[raw/website/projects/datafest-2023#5. Turning it on our own system|website/projects/datafest-2023.md › 5. Turning it on our own system]])
- Including the weak case made the strong case credible by demonstrating the scores separate cases that genuinely differ. ([[raw/website/projects/datafest-2023#6. Why the failing example was the point|website/projects/datafest-2023.md › 6. Why the failing example was the point]])
