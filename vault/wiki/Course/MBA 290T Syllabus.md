# MBA 290T Syllabus

MBA 290T: Fundamental of Agentic AI (Haas School of Business, UC Berkeley, Fall 2026) is a seven-class course that goes from programming foundations to building and evaluating tool-using AI agents. It is graded on attendance (20%) and five assignments, and it expects students to use AI coding agents while being able to explain everything they submit.

## Key facts
- Fundamental of Agentic AI is not a survey course about AI, and it is not a prompt-engineering workshop. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Course Objective]])
- In seven sessions the course goes from “what is a variable” to building and evaluating a tool-using AI agent, covering programming foundations, full-stack software systems, machine learning, deep learning and transformers, LLM behavior and retrieval, and agent architecture. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Course Objective]])
- The course targets Level 2 fluency: you do not need a PhD to reason about AI systems, but knowing how to use ChatGPT is not a differentiator either. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Course Objective]])
- A student who successfully completes this course will be able to read, modify, test, and debug real code, and explain what a program does before running it. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Learning Outcomes]])
- The course is assessed entirely through attendance and five graded assignments. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Class Schedule]])
- Attendance is required and is worth 20% of the grade. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Attendance]])
- Assignments 1–4 are the end-of-class deliverables for Classes 2 through 5. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Graded Assignments]])
- Assignment 5 is the course capstone and spans Classes 6 and 7. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Graded Assignments]])
- Every assignment is scored out of 10 using the same broad framework. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › How Assignments Are Graded]])
- Each assignment is scored on deliverable quality (4 points), testing & evaluation (3) and working result (3). ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › How Assignments Are Graded]])
- The final grade weights attendance 20%, Assignments 1–4 50% (lowest score dropped) and Assignment 5 30%. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Grading]])
- Class 7 is on Tue 10/6 and covers Multi-Agent Systems, Ops & Safety. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Class Schedule]])
- The focus is conceptual fluency that survives the tools. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Course Objective]])
- Assignments are individual. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Graded Assignments]])
- Students are expected to use AI coding agents, and also to understand and be able to explain everything they submit. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › Graded Assignments]])
- The distinction this course cares about is not “did a human or an AI write this.” It is “does the person submitting it understand the system well enough to be responsible for it.” ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › AI / LLM Policy]])
- Every assignment is designed to be built with an AI coding agent. ([[raw/course/syllabus.html|course/syllabus.html › AI / LLM Policy]])
